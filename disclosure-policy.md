# Reporting a security concern privately

Contact [security@mkdl.jp](mailto:security@mkdl.jp), the address listed in [MKDL security.txt](https://mkdl.jp/.well-known/security.txt). Start with the affected public repository/version and a short non-sensitive description. Ask for a suitable private channel before sending secrets, customer data or confidential evidence.

Once a channel and scope are agreed, provide observed behavior, expected behavior, reproduction using synthetic data where possible, impact assumptions and the evidence supporting them. Separate a confirmed issue from a suspected attack path.

Do not test a real customer environment, private Hub instance or third-party service on the basis of this policy. It supplies a reporting route, not testing authorization, a legal safe harbor, a reward program or a promised response deadline.

Documentation corrections with no sensitive detail may use public issues. Coordinate disclosure of a sensitive finding privately, including affected scope, remediation verification and what may be published.
